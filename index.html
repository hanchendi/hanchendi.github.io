About

Chendi Han received his BS degree in Physics from Cuiying Honors College, Lanzhou University, in 2017. He began his research in quantum chaos in Professor Liang Huang's group.

He received his MS and PhD degrees in Electrical Engineering from the School of Electrical, Computer and Energy Engineering, Arizona State University, in 2019 and 2022, respectively. His PhD research on graphene, quantum chaos, and machine learning was conducted under Professor Ying-Cheng Lai.

In 2023, he joined Cleveland Clinic as an MRI Research Engineer. His current work focuses on computational and machine-learning methods for functional MRI and their applications to neurological disease.

Research interests

His current research interests are fMRI data analysis, optimization, machine learning, and clinical applications to Alzheimer's disease and Parkinson's disease.
